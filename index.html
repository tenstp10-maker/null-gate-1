HOW TO PLAY · NULL GATE 1.3 MP-4.2

Win

  • 3 vs 3. Pick a map on the home screen: Planets, Arena (the forts) or Core Arena; every mode plays on all three. Your team scores 1 point a second while only your team is inside a live hill. If both teams are inside, nobody scores there.
  • First to 150 wins. At 6:00 the higher score wins.

Every mode

  • Hills circle a body, one lap every 37 s, then move to the next: on Planets, A rock → B gas → C ice; on Arena, the core inside each fort; on Core Arena, the core (A), then beacons B and C. Gold dots trace the live orbit; the gold ring on your screen points to the live hill.
  • The live hill has gravity: inside, it carries you along and pulls you to its middle, so you can stop steering and fight. Steer to move or leave.
  • When the hill moves on, anyone still inside is slung out along its path.
  • Each live hill has a moon and asteroids circling it. Planets, moons and asteroids are solid: they block shots and movement.
  • Each team spawns on its home planet (Planets) or its home wall (Arena, Core Arena).
  • Planets pull on shots. A bolt flying past a planet curves toward it: more the closer it passes and the longer it flies, so Power's 80 m shots bend most. Gas giant B pulls hardest, then rock A, then ice C; moons pull a little. The arena cores and beacons are small but dense, pulling about as hard as rock A. When a planet will bend your shot, a dotted aim arc shows where it goes (red end = it hits the planet). Aim assist and bots allow for the bend too.

Eclipse

  • A star lights one side of each planet. For about 14 s of every lap the hill passes through the planet's shadow.
  • In shadow the hill scores 2 points a second. The orbit dots dim there.
  • Enemies in shadow are hidden beyond 15 m, unless they fired in the last second. Bots play by the same rule.

Twin Orbit

  • Two hills circle the same planet in opposite directions (A1 and A2). Each scores 1 point a second, so holding both scores 2.
  • They cross every 18.5 s. The pair stays two laps (74 s), then moves to the next planet.
  • The second hill shows a second marker and a second status chip under the score.

Ring Run

  • Hill B's body (planet B, fort B's core or beacon B) has a debris ring. Its hill flies through the ring twice a lap.
  • Ring rocks are solid cover. A rock breaks after 3 Power hits or 5 hits from Shield or Speed. All rocks come back when the hill moves on.
  • The A and C bodies have thin dust rings (look only).

Getting hit

  • Each hit takes 1 shield cell. A hit with no cells left freezes you.
  • Frozen soldiers respawn after 4 s, fully shielded, at the safest spawn on their home planet or wall.
  • Cells come back one at a time once you go 3 s without being hit.

Specialties

  • Shield · Bulwark: 3 shield cells. Slowest, shortest range (34 m).
  • Speed · Comet: fastest, with a dash. 1 cell, 40 m range.
  • Power · Lancer: 80 m range, and every hit knocks off a whole shield. Slow to fire, 1 cell.

Controls

  • Left thumb: drag anywhere on the left side to fly where you look.
  • Right thumb: drag to look. Hold FIRE to shoot. ▲ / ▼ thrust up and down. DASH (Speed only) bursts forward.
  • Keyboard (default): W A S D move forward, back and sideways on the level plane (looking up or down doesn't tilt it). E moves up, C moves down. Mouse to look (click to lock the pointer), click or F to fire, Shift to dash, Esc to pause.
  • Controls: tap Controls on the home screen or in the pause menu to change any movement, fire or dash binding (keys, mouse buttons or the wheel, two per action), the look speed and invert look. Settings are saved on this device.
  • Menu: step 1 picks the map and game mode, step 2 your team, specialty, the bots' specialties and difficulty. The icons at the top right open How to play, Maps, HUD, Controls and Sound.
  • HUD: tap HUD on the home screen, or Customize HUD in the pause menu, to change the crosshair, HUD size and opacity, what the HUD shows, and the touch buttons (size, opacity, left-handed, and drag to move). Settings are saved on this device.

MAPS

Every mode plays on any of three World Builder maps (seed 1). Pick one on the home screen, next to Deploy.

  • Planets (140 × 90 × 240 m): two home planets, the rock (A), gas (B) and ice (C) hill planets, and three small moons. Teams spawn on their home planets.
  • Arena (80 × 56 × 120 m): the Hardpoint arena with its three hollow forts. A small, dense core sits in each fort, and the hill circles the fort 13 m out. The fort walls are cover. Teams spawn on their home walls.
  • Core Arena (80 × 56 × 140 m): the enclosed arena with chamfered edges, gate barriers and rotated prisms, cubes, pyramids and pillars. Hill A circles the core; B and C circle two beacons. Teams spawn on their home walls.
  • Eclipse: the star sits behind one wall; each hill body casts a shadow away from it.
  • Twin Orbit: six hills, two per body.
  • Ring Run: 150 debris rocks ring hill B's body.

Loose blocks and pieces that a moving hill or its satellites would sweep through are left out, so each map keeps its walls, forts and barriers but loses a few loose pieces. Custom World Builder maps come back with Hardpoint.
